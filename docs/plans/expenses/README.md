# Expense management planning

Updated: October 25, 2026.

This folder preserves the 45 general feature ideas and 15 advanced concepts discussed for Tripraft's personal expense and group travel experience. All 60 ideas have one combined priority ranking, from highest to lowest.

## How to read the ranking

The ordering is a product judgment balancing frequency of use, user benefit, implementation effort, dependencies, and potential differentiation. It is not a measured demand score or a delivery commitment. Combining both lists changes their earlier standalone order.

The recommended first five additions are budgets with alerts, recurring transactions, imports with previews, duplicate detection, and multiple payers. Receipt scanning follows these foundations. The advanced simulator and agreement-aware splitting can build on budgets, saved split rules, and itinerary-linked costs.

## Scope and status

- These are proposals, not claims that the features have been implemented.
- The current planning scope is the web application, without paid-customer restrictions or a mobile-app requirement.
- Existing expense splitting, member analytics, group invitations, and history provide foundations for the proposed extensions. Planning entries describe the additional behavior.
- Original list references are preserved: G01-G45 identify the general ideas, and A01-A15 identify the advanced concepts. They are identifiers, not the combined priority rank.
- Advanced concepts are potential differentiators. Worldwide novelty has not been established; competitor research and user validation are still needed.
- Estimates, simulated balances, proposed corrections, and expected refunds must remain distinguishable from saved transactions and confirmed receipts. Users review consequential changes before they are applied.
